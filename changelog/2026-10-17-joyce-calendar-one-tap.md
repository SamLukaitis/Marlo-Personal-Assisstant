Adding the family calendar to an iPhone is now one tap: Joyce sends a link, you tap it, then tap Subscribe.

Before, Joyce sent the raw calendar address and people had to copy it into Settings → Calendar →
Accounts → Add Subscribed Calendar. Now she sends a link to a small page. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac
it opens the Calendar app's "Subscribe?" prompt straight away; on Android or a computer it shows
"Add to iPhone or Mac Calendar" and "Add to Google Calendar" buttons. The calendar is called
"Family (Joyce)" and suggests a rose-pink colour to calendar apps (Apple may or may not use it;
anyone can change it).

It's still a subscription, read-only and updating by itself; Google Calendar only refreshes
subscribed calendars every few hours. Apple has no way for an app to add a calendar to iCloud
directly. Connecting Google properly (instant updates, and busy/free from members' own calendars)
is a separate decision — see "Timezones, reminders and the calendar" in
`feature-to-do/joyce-family-assistant.md`.
