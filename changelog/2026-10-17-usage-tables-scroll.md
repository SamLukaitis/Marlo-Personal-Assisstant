The Portal's Usage tab no longer turns into a very long page: its two tables now scroll inside their own boxes.

## What changed

The "Day by day" and "Recent calls" tables on Usage (Marlowe's and Joyce's portals) could run to
hundreds of rows, so reaching the bottom meant scrolling the whole page a long way. Each table is
now capped at 480px (or 60% of the screen, whichever is smaller) and scrolls on its own. The column
headings stay pinned at the top of each box while you scroll.
